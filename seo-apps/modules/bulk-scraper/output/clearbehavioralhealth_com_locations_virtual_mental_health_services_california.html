What to expect in our online treatment programs

Our thoughtfully designed, in-network mental health programs are delivered affordably online across California.

Virtual Intensive Outpatient Program (Virtual IOP)

Our Virtual IOP offers flexible, remote mental health care ideal for working adults, students, or those needing accessible support. Treatment typically includes virtual therapy for 3 hours a day, 3 days a week, over 8 to 12 weeks—providing structure and healing without disrupting daily life.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Online group & individual therapy
  • Weekly psychiatric check-ins
  • Flexible scheduling (day or evening tracks)
  • Family involvement via virtual sessions
  • Privacy and HIPAA-compliant platform
Learn more

LGBTQIA+ Virtual Intensive Outpatient Program (Virtual IOP)

Our LGBTQIA+ Virtual Intensive Outpatient Program provides structured, affirming mental health care for individuals navigating mental health challenges in a safe, inclusive, and identity-affirming environment.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Individual one-on-one therapy with LGBTQIA+-affirming clinicians
  • Therapist-led virtual group therapy in a safe, validating space
  • Evidence-based treatments, including CBT, DBT, and mindfulness-based care
  • Trauma-informed and queer-affirming clinical approach
  • Support for stress, anxiety, depression, identity-related challenges, and life transitions
  • Gender-affirming care letters, when clinically appropriate
  • Psychiatric evaluation and medication management, as needed
  • Guidance with medical leave and short-term disability paperwork, when eligible
Learn more

Evening Virtual IOP

Our Evening Virtual IOP provides structured, evidence-based mental health care for adults who need consistent support while maintaining work, school, or daytime responsibilities.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Online group & individual therapy
  • Weekly psychiatric check-ins
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Family involvement via virtual sessions
  • Privacy and HIPAA-compliant platform
