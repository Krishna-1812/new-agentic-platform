          Pay My Bill

          Care Credit

          Care Credit is a third-party healthcare credit card that helps finance healthcare related costs. Care Credit extends special financing options that you can’t get when using your Visa or MasterCard. Whether you use your healthcare credit card to cover your deductible, or to pay for treatments and procedures not covered by insurance, CareCredit helps make them possible today.

          LEARN MORE

          Gentle Dental accepts Essential Dental Plan which allows members to receive the dental care they need at the savings they can afford. Get instant access to significant savings on all dental services plus free exams and x-rays. Choose individual, two-individual, or family coverage and enjoy peace of mind knowing you can maintain your oral health. The Essential Dental Plan is not insurance.

          Gentle Dental partners with Sunbit to pay for treatment over time. Sunbit allows patients to split their treatment cost into monthly payments for up to 72 months. Over 85% of patients who apply are approved.

          Frequently Asked Questions

          Do you offer new patient discounts or offers in the Concord Gentle Dental Office?

          Yes, we do! We are always accepting new patients. Our $57 New Patient Offer is a great introduction to our practice. It includes an exam, x-rays, cleaning, and treatment plan. Schedule an appointment today.

          Do you offer weekend appointments?

          Yes! Gentle Dental Concord Hospital is open on Saturdays and is also open in the evenings. Call today to request an appointment.

          Does the dental office in Concord offer pediatric dental services?

          Yes, we do. Our dentists and dental hygienists have the training and expertise to meet the unique dental needs of your infants, children, adolescents, and persons with special health care needs.

          How to keep my teeth healthy during COVID-19?

          Keeping up with a good oral care routine at home is your first line of defense. Remember to brush 2x a day for at least two minutes and floss daily. Limit the amount of sticky, sugary foods you consume and drink plenty of water. Of course, visiting the dentist is still an important part of your oral care routine. Gentle Dental Concord Hospital and all of our Gentle Dental dental offices are safely seeing patients with all requirements in place.

          Do I go to the dentist during a pandemic?

          Yes. You should visit the dentist. All of our Gentle Dental offices, including Gentle Dental Concord Hospital are safely seeing patients with all necessary precautions in place including temperature checks, health screenings, and PPE for all staff members. Small dental problems can turn into a more serious problem if not addressed quickly so don’t put off care.

          Is the Gentle Dental Concord Hospital safe during the COVID-19 pandemic?

          Visiting the dentist has always been safe and now is no exception. We implement the highest level of infection control and have implemented all new requirements from the ADA and CDC including n95 masks and all necessary PPE.

          What areas does Gentle Dental Concord serve?

          We provide extensive care and serve patients in Concord, Pembroke, Bow, Hopkinton and Loudon and anyone from beyond these areas who seeks Gentle Dental care.

          What does Gentle Dental Concord offer? Why choose Gentle Dental Concord?

          Gentle Dental Concord is a friendly dentist office in Concord that provides comprehensive dental care to its patients. Your search for an affordable dentist office near home ends here. At Gentle Dental, patients receive award-winning dental care from the best dentists in the neighborhood. With the latest in dental technology including digital low radiation x-rays, 3D scanning and intraoral cameras, Gentle Dental Concord is the best dentist in Concord. Come visit our dentist office in Concord today!

          Does Gentle Dental Concord provide emergency dental service?

          Gentle Dental Concord sees emergency dental cases the same day as it can be acutely painful and discomforting for the patient. Gentle Dental Concord provides emergency dental services and tends to dental emergencies such as chipped, cracked or missing teeth, tooth or Gum Abscess, toothache, mouth pain, bitten/cut tongue or cheek, broken braces wires or brackets, jaw pain and other such dental emergencies cases. Gentle Dental Concord provides emergency care that can help alleviate your pain and discomfort and help prevent permanent damage or bone loss. Just call to schedule a dental emergency appointment today.

          How can I make a same-day appointment with a Dentist in Concord Hospital?

          Call our office for a same-day appointment at (603) 224-5424

          Does Gentle Dental Concord Hospital accept dental plans?

          Yes, Gentle Dental Concord Hospital does accept Essential Dental Plan and Care Credit.

          Can I get Invisalign at Gentle Dental Concord Hospital?

          Yes, we offer Invisalign treatment at Gentle Dental Concord Hospital.

          Does Gentle Dental Concord Hospital accept medicare?

          Gentle Dental Concord Hospital accepts most dental insurance plans but does not currently accept Medicare.

          What are the different services offered by Gentle Dental Concord Hospital?

          Gentle Dental Concord Hospital is a multi-specialty dental practice seeing patients for all phases of dental care from children to adults, general dentistry to specialty care.

          Does Gentle Dental Concord Hospital offer dental services for kids?

          Yes, Gentle Dental Concord Hospital offers dental services for kids.
