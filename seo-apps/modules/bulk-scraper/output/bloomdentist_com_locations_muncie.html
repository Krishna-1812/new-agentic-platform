Meet Dr. Madison Whybrew

Dr. Whybrew is a general dentist dedicated to preventive care, personalized treatment, and building lasting trust with patients. She believes dentistry is about more than teeth—it’s about listening, education, and helping every patient feel confident in their smile.

Meet Dr. Katelyn Brown-Minor

Dr. Katelyn Brown-Minor earned degrees in Biochemistry and Spanish from IU East before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the IU School of Dentistry. She is passionate about service and reducing barriers to care, and she strives to create a comfortable, supportive environment—especially for patients with dental anxiety.

Meet Dr. Jamie Childers

Dr. Childers is a Muncie native and experienced dentist committed to high-quality, patient-centered care. He earned his dental degree from Midwestern University after completing undergraduate and graduate studies at Ball State University. Dr. Childers provides a wide range of treatments—from routine fillings to crowns, bridges, and advanced implant dentistry, including All-on-Four. He focuses on helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident at every visit.

Meet Dr. Virgil Lungu

Dr. Lungu is a dentist with a strong passion for oral surgery, with a particular focus on wisdom teeth removals and dental implants. He takes pride in guiding patients through procedures that often cause anxiety, making every effort to ensure they feel comfortable, informed, and at ease throughout their entire visit. For Dr. Lungu, there is nothing more rewarding than transforming a stressful dental experience into a positive one and helping patients leave feeling relieved and confident.
