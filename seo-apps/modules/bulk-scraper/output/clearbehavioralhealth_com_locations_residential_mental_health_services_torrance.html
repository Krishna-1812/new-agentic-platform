Featured Programs

Our thoughtfully designed, in-network mental health and addiction programs are delivered affordably in a comfortable setting nearby, or online across California.

Residential Mental Health

Our residential program provides 24/7 immersive care for individuals with severe or persistent mental health needs. With an average stay of 30 days, clients receive intensive psychiatric support, a safe and supportive environment, and structured daily programming to promote stability and healing.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Psychiatric evaluations & medication management
  • Individual and group therapy
  • 24/7 clinical supervision
  • Psychoeducation groups
  • Holistic therapies (yoga, mindfulness, creative expression)

Virtual Intensive Outpatient Program (Virtual IOP)

Our Virtual IOP offers flexible, remote mental health care ideal for working adults, students, or those needing accessible support. Treatment typically includes virtual therapy for 3 hours a day, 3 days a week, over 8 to 12 weeks—providing structure and healing without disrupting daily life.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Online group & individual therapy
  • Weekly psychiatric check-ins
  • Flexible scheduling (day or evening tracks)
  • Family involvement via virtual sessions
  • Privacy and HIPAA-compliant platform

Residential Detox Program

Our medically supervised detox program provides 24/7 care in a safe, supportive environment for people withdrawing from drugs or alcohol. With continuous monitoring and evidence-based safety protocols, we help manage withdrawal symptoms and prepare clients for the next step in recovery.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Medical supervision and vital monitoring
  • Medication-assisted withdrawal management
  • 24/7 nursing support
  • Emotional support & therapeutic check-ins
  • Discharge planning & next level of care coordination

Residential Rehab Program

Our residential rehab program offers full-time, structured care for individuals recovering from substance use, either following detox or as a primary treatment. With an average stay of 30+ days, clients receive daily therapy, medical support, relapse prevention planning, and holistic services in a supportive environment.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Daily therapy sessions
  • Group and individual counseling
  • Relapse prevention planning
  • Family therapy
  • Nutritional support & wellness activities

Dual Diagnosis Program

Our Dual Diagnosis Program is designed for individuals navigating both mental health and substance use disorders. Through integrated, whole-person care that combines clinical therapy and psychiatric support, we help clients address the root causes of both conditions and build a path to lasting recovery.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Psychiatric evaluations and med management
  • Co-occurring group therapy
  • Addiction counseling
  • Trauma-informed care
  • Discharge & aftercare planning

Outpatient Rehab

Our Outpatient Rehab offers flexible addiction treatment for individuals who don’t require 24/7 residential care. With options such as Intensive Outpatient (IOP) and Partial Hospitalization (PHP), this level of support is ideal for those transitioning from inpatient care or managing recovery alongside daily responsibilities.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Weekly therapy sessions
  • Group therapy and peer support
  • Medication support
  • Case management   

Teen Program

Our teen program provides age-appropriate care for adolescents facing emotional, behavioral, or mental health challenges who don’t require residential support. We offer IOP and PHP options, structured for teens who can live at home but need ongoing support for mental health and substance use issues. We focus on balancing school life, family support, and integrated therapy to promote healthy growth and recovery.

Services Provided at Clear

  • Weekly therapy sessions (individual and group)
  • Case management and treatment coordination
  • Peer support and group therapy
  • Family involvement and counseling   
  • Relapse prevention and aftercare planning

Frequently Asked Questions

What is residential mental health treatment in Torrance, CA?

Residential mental health treatment in Torrance provides 24/7 support in a safe, home-like setting where clients receive structured therapy, clinical care, and holistic support. It’s designed for individuals who need more than outpatient care but don’t require hospitalization.

How long is a residential mental health stay in Torrance?

Length of stay varies depending on each client’s needs. On average, residential treatment lasts 30–60 days, though some clients may benefit from longer care for sustained recovery.

What’s the difference between inpatient and residential mental health treatment?

Inpatient treatment is typically hospital-based, highly structured, and focused on crisis stabilization. Residential treatment is less clinical, provided in a supportive home environment, and emphasizes long-term healing through therapy, skill-building, and daily structure.

What mental health conditions do you treat at your Torrance location?

Our Torrance residential program treats a wide range of conditions, including depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, PTSD and trauma, OCD, and co-occurring disorders.

Do your Torrance mental health services accept insurance?

Yes. Clear Behavioral Health works with many major insurance providers. Our admissions team can verify your benefits and explain coverage options before treatment begins.

Is family therapy included in your residential programs?

Yes. Family involvement is a core part of treatment. We provide family therapy sessions to improve communication, strengthen relationships, and support long-term recovery.
