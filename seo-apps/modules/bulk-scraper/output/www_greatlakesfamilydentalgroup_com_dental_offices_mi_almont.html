Great Lakes Family Dental - Almont

Our Great Lakes Family Dental practice is located in Almont, Michigan right off the busy M-53 highway connecting Detroit to the Michigan “thumb” area. Nestled in a quaint village in Lapeer County, our practice has been a proud member of the Almont Raider's rural history for over 13 years. Patients chose our office for our highly skilled staff and a strong reputation for patient-first dental care. Our practice offers a wide range of dental services including orthodontics, Invisalign, and complimentary nitrous sedation. The warm staff and loyal patient base help to put the family in family dentistry. Now accepting Delta PPO! 

Dental Services

Our dentists and dental specialists provide a full range of dental services in a comfortable welcoming setting. Preventive care such as digital x-rays and cleanings and more advanced services like braces and root canals are available in-house with no need for a referral.

Paying for Your Care

Our goal is to ensure you get the quality dental care you need. Great Lakes Family Dental welcomes most dental insurance plans. We also accept Essential Dental Plan, Care Credit and a new Pay-Over-Time financing option from Sunbit. During your visit, our trained staff can assist you in meeting your financial obligations.

All Great Lakes Family Dental practices accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, Personal Checks and Money Orders.


Accepted Dental Insurance

We are in-network providers with most major dental insurance companies. Call us to confirm coverage.

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts Essential Dental Plan which allows members to receive the dental care they need at the savings they can afford. Get instant access to significant savings on all dental services plus free exams and x-rays. Choose individual, two-individual, or family coverage and enjoy peace of mind knowing you can maintain your oral health. The Essential Dental Plan is not insurance.

Care Credit is a third-party healthcare credit card that helps finance healthcare related costs. Care Credit extends special financing options that you can’t get when using your Visa or MasterCard. Frequently Asked Questions

Does the dental office in Almont offer pediatric dental services?

Yes, we do. Our dentists and dental hygienists have the training and expertise to meet the unique dental needs of your infants, children, adolescents, and persons with special health care needs.

How to keep my teeth healthy during COVID-19?

Keeping up with a good oral care routine at home is your first line of defense. Remember to brush 2x a day for at least two minutes and floss daily. Limit the amount of sticky, sugary foods you consume and drink plenty of water. Of course, visiting the dentist is still an important part of your oral care routine. Great Lakes Family Dental Almont and all of our Great Lakes Family Dental offices are safely seeing patients with all guidelines in place.

Do I go to the dentist during a pandemic?

Yes. You should visit the dentist. All of our Great Lakes Family Dental offices, including Great Lakes Family Dental Almont are safely seeing patients with all necessary precautions in place including temperature checks, health screenings, and PPE for all staff members. Small dental problems can turn into a more serious problem if not addressed quickly so don’t put off care.

Is the Great Lakes Family Dental Almont safe during the COVID-19 pandemic?

Visiting the dentist has always been safe and now is no exception. We implement the highest level of infection control and have implemented all guidelines from the ADA and CDC including n95 masks and all necessary PPE.

What areas does Great Lakes Family Dental Almont serve?

We provide extensive care and serve patients in Almont, Lexington, and Winchester and anyone from beyond these areas who seek Great Lakes Family Dental care.

What does Great Lakes Family Dental Almont offer? Why choose Great Lakes Family Dental Almont?

At Great Lakes Family Dental, patients receive award-winning dental care from the best dentists in the neighborhood. With the latest in dental technology including digital low radiation x-rays, 3D scanning and intraoral cameras, Great Lakes Family Dental Almont is the best dentist in Almont. Come visit our dentist office in Almont today!

Does Great Lakes Family Dental Almont provide emergency dental service?

Great Lakes Family Dental Almont sees emergency dental cases the same day as it can be acutely painful and discomforting for the patient. Great Lakes Family Dental Almont provides emergency dental services and tends to dental emergencies such as chipped, cracked or missing teeth, a tooth or Gum Abscess, toothache, mouth pain, bitten/cut tongue or cheek, broken braces wires or brackets, jaw pain and other such dental emergencies cases. Great Lakes Family Dental Almont provides emergency care that can help alleviate your pain and discomfort and help prevent permanent damage or bone loss. Just call to schedule a dental emergency appointment today.

Can I pay over time for my dental care?

Yes, you can now pay over time with Sunbit, which allows patients to split their bill into monthly payments up to 72 months with an approval rate of over 85% of patients, even those with lower credit scores. It’s an exciting program that helps patients get the treatment they need for a healthy, confident smile. Learn more about how you can get your dental care today and pay over time.

Do you accept any discount dental plans?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts Essential Dental, a dental savings plan that provides immediate savings on dental care for you and your family. The plan has no waiting period and no annual maximums. Exams and x-rays are free. Individual, 2 individuals and family memberships are available starting at $99. For more information and to enroll, visit essentialdentalplan.com. Essential Dental is not insurance.

Will my insurance cover cosmetic dental care?

Insurance coverage and out-of-pocket expenses for cosmetic treatment vary. Our practice will work with your insurance provider to verify coverage and will provide you with different treatment options to achieve your cosmetic goals. We will review treatment options with you and help you find a payment option for your needs and your budget.

I’m worried about the cost of dental care I need. What are my options

At Great Lakes Family Dental, we welcome new patients at $57 for an exam, all necessary x-rays, a cleaning and treatment plan. This is a great way to get to know the practice. We also accept Essential Dental, a dental savings plan that allows members to have the dental care they need at the savings they can afford. And now you can pay over time with Sunbit, which allows patients to split their bill into monthly payments up to 72 months with an approval rate of over 85% of patients, even those with lower credit scores. Please call your practice to speak to a patient care representative about your payment options.

Does health insurance cover dental work?

Health insurance and dental insurance are two different types of coverage. Some procedures such as oral surgery may be covered under your health insurance. Call your office and speak with a Patient Care Representative to learn more.

Do I need dental insurance to get braces?

While many plans provide Orthodontic benefits, you do not need dental insurance. Braces are available to all patients regardless of dental insurance! We offer several payment options including No Money Down and monthly payment plans with autopay.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

Most dental insurance plans cover dental implants. Each plan is different so contact your dental insurance company for full details.

Does Great Lakes Family Dental accept Medicare?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts most dental insurance plans but does not currently accept Medicare.

Does Great Lakes Family Dental accept Medicaid?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts most dental insurance plans but does not currently accept Medicaid.
