About our sober living program

New Life House’s sober living program combines multiple treatment approaches in one supportive setting, helping you strengthen recovery, rebuild your life, and gain the skills for lasting independence.

Core residential program at New Life House

Program details:

  • Morning meditation
  • Shared breakfast with fellow residents

  • Substance use and mental health treatment for residents in early recovery (in partnership with Clear Behavioral Health)
  • Life-skills programming, including:
  • Financial management
  • Time management
  • Communication and interpersonal skills
  • Attendance at outside recovery community events and meetings

Substance use treatment program

Our Substance Use Treatment Program through our sister program, Clear Behavioral Health, offers personalized therapy, recovery support, and structure so you can regain control of your life and move forward with confidence.

Program details:

  • A comprehensive approach designed to help you manage and overcome addiction
  • Personalized care through one-on-one therapy, group therapy, and medication management when needed
  • Weekly individual sessions to explore your triggers, challenges, and goals
  • Daily peer group therapy where clients learn from one another and build accountability
  • Holistic therapy through yoga, meditation, and music therapy to reconnect you with your body

Outpatient drug treatment program

Our Outpatient Drug Treatment Programs provide structured, evidence-based care for individuals seeking recovery while continuing work, school, or family responsibilities. Through our partnership with Clear Behavioral Health, we offer personalized treatment that supports long-term sobriety and emotional well-being.

Program details:

  • Flexible outpatient care focused on drug addiction recovery
  • Individualized treatment plans including therapy, group counseling, and medication support when appropriate
  • Weekly one-on-one therapy to address triggers and recovery goals
  • Regular peer group sessions to build accountability and community
  • Holistic therapies such as meditation, yoga, and wellness activities

Family therapy program

Our Family Therapy Program strengthens family relationships and creates a healthier support system for you during recovery.

Program details:

  • Weekly family therapy to rebuild trust, improve communication, and resolve conflicts
  • Biweekly group family therapy to connect with other families, build community, and trust within the group
  • Helps families create a supportive, stable environment for long-term recovery
  • Empowers everyone involved to move forward together

Educational program

Program details:

  • Weekly learning activities, including classes, workshops, vocational training, and life skills sessions
  • Support for GED/college coursework, certifications, and academic pathways
  • Training in time management, organization, communication, and financial literacy

Alumni program

How sober living for men works

The process of joining one of our sober living homes is simple, and thoughtfully designed to help you feel comfortable and prepared. Here’s how it works:

1. Reach out

Contact our admissions team at 424-722-4262 to discuss your needs and determine if our sober living environment is a suitable fit.

2. Assessment

We’ll conduct a brief assessment to understand your background, recovery goals, and readiness for structured support.

3. Move-in & orientation

Once you’re ready, you’ll join the house, meet your peers, and get familiar with the routines and expectations.

4. Daily structure

You’ll follow a schedule that includes meetings, therapy (when applicable), curfews, and regular drug testing.

5. Build community

As you settle in, you’ll participate in group activities, house responsibilities, and peer support.

6. Recovery integration

Over time, you’ll develop healthy routines, build life and coping skills, pursue education or employment, and regain independence with a strong support network in place.

Frequently asked questions

How long do clients typically stay in sober living?

Most clients stay between 3–12 months, depending on their goals, progress, and the level of support needed. Some choose a shorter stay to reinforce early recovery skills, while others benefit from a longer, structured environment as they transition back into everyday life. At New Life House, your stay is tailored to what will best support your long term success.

What’s the difference between sober living and rehab?

Rehab focuses on detox and intensive treatment, usually in a clinical or inpatient setting. Sober living, on the other hand, gives you a safe, substance-free home where you can apply what you learned in treatment to real life. It’s about building routines, practicing responsibility, strengthening coping skills, and gaining independence, with support from a sober community.

Do I need to complete treatment before entering a sober living home?

Most clients enter sober living after completing detox or primary treatment, but requirements vary based on your situation and clinical recommendations. If you’re unsure, our team can guide you toward the level of care that fits your needs and ensures a safe, stable transition through our partner organization, Clear Behavioral Health.

Can clients work or go to school while living in sober living?

Yes, and it’s often encouraged. Sober living provides structure and accountability so you can return to work, school, or volunteer activities while still having a strong recovery foundation. You’ll have house expectations and curfews, but plenty of room to build a healthy, independent routine.

What is the environment like inside the house?

You can expect a supportive, community oriented atmosphere where everyone is working toward the same goal: long-term sobriety. There’s structure, daily routines, peer accountability, and a sense of belonging. Clients cook, attend meetings, participate in house activities, and support each other through challenges and wins.

What kind of support does the staff provide?

You’ll benefit from a team of experienced health experts, mentors, and counselors who offer guidance, weekly check ins, life skills support, and recovery focused programming. The goal is to help you stay grounded, motivated, and connected while you build the confidence to thrive on your own.

Is sober living suitable for young adults?

Absolutely. Many sober living programs, including New Life House, are specifically designed for teens and young adults ages 18 and up, and offer age-appropriate support, structure, and community. Clients benefit from being surrounded by peers who understand what they’re going through and who are equally committed to recovery.

Can family members stay involved?

Yes. Family involvement can be a powerful part of recovery, and New Life House encourages ongoing communication, family therapy, and educational resources. The goal is to help loved ones understand the recovery process and support you in healthy, constructive ways.
