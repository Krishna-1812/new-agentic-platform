Services we offer

Our compassionate team at Neuro Wellness Spa works with you to determine the specialized mental health treatment that will work best for you. We are here with you every step of the way on your journey.

Psychiatry services

Our Pasadena psychiatrists offer personalized mental health evaluations and treatment plans for anxiety, depression, trauma, and related conditions. We offer flexible scheduling, integrated therapy and psychiatry, and a supportive, outcome-focused approach.

Explore more

Adolescent psychiatry

Psychiatric care at Neuro Wellness Spa is available for clients seeking support with anxiety, mood disorders, or stress. Our teen services are designed to help young people build coping skills, manage symptoms, and improve their well-being through personalized treatment.

Explore more

Psychotherapy

Therapy sessions are available for clients seeking support with anxiety, mood disorders, or stress. Our licensed clinicians use evidence-based modalities such as CBT and mindfulness in a calming setting for our clients.

Explore more

TMS therapy

TMS is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment for depression and other mental health conditions. Our Pasadena location offers TMS therapy, which is ideal for those who haven’t found relief for depression and related mood disorders through traditional treatments.

Frequently asked questions

Are there psychiatrists in Pasadena who offer telehealth appointments?

Both in-person and virtual sessions are offered at our Pasadena location. Give us a call today and we’ll work out a treatment plan that works for you and your circumstances whether it’s an in-person visit to our office or a virtual session with our mental health care professionals.

Which psychiatrists specialize in OCD or anxiety in Pasadena?

Our goal is to align you with a therapist that fits your condition, treatment requirements, and personality to help produce the most impactful, longest-lasting outcome possible.

What are the price ranges and options for affordable care in Pasadena?

Mental health care is often covered by insurance, and how much you can expect to pay will be dependent on the recommended care and your coverage. We’re experts at helping you navigate your coverage – give us a call today and our team will help you understand what you can expect to have covered by insurance and what to expect in out-of-pocket costs once we understand what type of treatment you’re seeking.

How quickly can I get an appointment with a psychiatrist in Pasadena?

Many patients are seen within 24 hours of reaching out to us at Neuro Wellness Spa. We understand that a mental health challenge is an urgent need, not something to be handled just when it’s convenient or fits into a schedule. If we cannot see you within 24 hours at our Pasadena office, we’ll schedule you as soon as possible or may be able to accommodate you at another of our locations around the Los Angeles area.
