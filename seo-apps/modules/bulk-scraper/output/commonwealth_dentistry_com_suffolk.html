Meet Our Suffolk Dentists

Dr. Surender Tippireddy

Surender Tippireddy, DDS, was inspired to pursue dentistry after a personal dental experience in childhood. His passion for working with his hands and a strong educational foundation from his parents led him to this profession, allowing him to serve others.

Dr. Tippireddy is originally from Secunderabad in Southern India. After graduating as a dentist in India, Dr. Tippireddy moved to the United States and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Tufts Dental School. He also served in the Army Reserve for 12 years, achieving the rank of Major before being honorably discharged.

Dr. Tippireddy specializes in selective molar endodontics, full arch implant placement and restorations, and third molar extractions. His dedication to continuous learning ensures he provides his patients with the highest quality of care.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Tippireddy enjoys spending time with his family, hiking, and watching his son play sports. He often volunteers to help coach his son’s teams. Dr. Tippireddy and his family love to travel and cook together. His commitment to patients and continuous professional growth make him a dedicated dental professional.
