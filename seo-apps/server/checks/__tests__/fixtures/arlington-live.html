Frequently Asked Questions

What should I consider when choosing a dentist in Arlington?

Look for a practice offering comprehensive, in-house treatments for patients of all ages. An ideal Arlington dentist provides modern technology, flexible scheduling, and a welcoming environment, just like our award-winning office on Mass. Ave.

Are there any dental offices in Arlington that offer reduced-cost services?

We strive to make dental care in Arlington affordable. We accept most insurance plans, CareCredit, and the Essential Dental Plan. We also provide flexible payment options and a New Patient Offer.

Does Gentle Dental Arlington offer cosmetic dentistry?

Yes, we provide a full spectrum of in-house treatments, including advanced cosmetic enhancements. Our experienced team is equipped to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile without the hassle of outside referrals.

What orthodontic procedures are offered at Gentle Dental Arlington?

We provide comprehensive orthodontic treatments, including traditional braces. Our skilled specialists provide these advanced services entirely in-house, without the need for outside referrals.

Are there any recommended dental practices near downtown Arlington, MA?

Yes! Our award-winning dental office in Arlington is conveniently located right on Mass. Ave. near City Hall. We have provided expert, multi-specialty care to the local community for over 30 years.

Yes, we accept same-day appointments for urgent issues like severe toothaches, chipped teeth, or broken braces. As your trusted Arlington dentist, we provide quick pain relief and prevent permanent damage. Call us at (781) 917-3456 today!

How can I book a same-day appointment with a dentist in Arlington?

Booking a same-day appointment is simple. Just call our office at (781) 917-3456 or book your visit online, and our dedicated team will find a convenient time to get you prompt dental care.

Can I get Invisalign® at Gentle Dental Arlington?

Yes, we provide Invisalign® clear aligners. This modern orthodontic solution provides a discreet, comfortable way to straighten your teeth without traditional metal braces.

Do you offer new patient discounts or offers at Gentle Dental Arlington?

Yes, we provide a New Patient Offer to help make your first visit more affordable. Our friendly staff is always available to discuss this offer, as well as our flexible financing options and the Essential Dental Plan.

Does Gentle Dental Arlington offer teeth whitening services?

Yes, we provide teeth whitening services at our Arlington office. Our team can go over your options and help you pick what works best for you. Call our dentist at (781) 917-3456 in Arlington, MA, to know more.

Where can I find pediatric dental care in Arlington, MA?

Right here! We provide comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages in a welcoming setting. If you need an Arlington kids dentist, our team ensures a comfortable, stress-free experience to help your children build healthy habits.

What should I look for when choosing a family dentist in Arlington, MA?
