How we contact people

How we reach people, and how they can say no.

Outreach

We write to people on behalf of our clients. If you received a message from us, this page explains why, and how to make sure it never happens again.

Who we write to

We write to people at businesses, about their work, when something they have done in public suggests a client of ours could help: a post about a problem, a question to their network, a published tender, news about their company, or a new role.

Where the information comes from

From what people and companies publish themselves: posts and comments on LinkedIn, community threads, tender notices, company news and event agendas, and from business contact data providers. Every lead we find carries a link to the public sign that found it. We do not use personal data that has nothing to do with someone’s work.

What we send

A short message, in the name of the client who would like to meet you, that says why they are writing. If we follow up, we stop as soon as you reply.

Saying no

Every email we send carries a one-click opt-out link. Using it stops all further email from us to that address, for every client we work with, for good. And we never start a second approach to the same person for the same client.

Questions

Reply to the message you received, or use the form on our Book a call page and mention outreach; a person will reply. How we handle personal information in general is in our Privacy Policy.
