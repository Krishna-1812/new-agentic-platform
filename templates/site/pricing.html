What counts as a meeting

Three checks, every time.

  1. The person fits

    They match the customer profile we agreed on the call.

  2. They said yes

    They accepted a meeting with you and it is on your calendar.

  3. It took place

    A no-show doesn’t count towards the promise.

What sets the number

Your market decides it.

  • How narrow your audience is, and where it is
  • How often its buyers show signs in public
  • How often they reply
  • Your deal size, and how much a meeting is worth to you
Against the alternatives

What others charge.

  • A first sales hire: about $108,600 in year one, salary and overheads included.
  • Pay-per-meeting agencies: commonly quoted at $75 to $500 a meeting.
  • Retainer plus meetings: commonly $2,000 to $4,000 a month plus $150 to $400 a meeting.

Ranges are from the providers’ own published guides, so treat them as ranges, not quotes.
