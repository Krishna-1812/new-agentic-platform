    {# ── How it watches ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
    02
    How it watches

    Nine steps, and only real changes get through.

    01

    Load

    A real browser, the same desktop size, language and time zone on every visit.

    02

    Hold still

    Animations frozen, cookie notices and chat bubbles hidden, lazy pictures loaded.

    03

    Read

    Every line of text with its place on the page, and a full-length picture.

    04

    Look twice

    A second picture seconds later shows what moves on its own: carousels, counters, ads.

    05

    Compare

    Text line by line, pictures pixel row by row, so a new banner does not make the rest of the page look changed.

    06

    Name it

    Every changed area is matched to what is in it: "the Sign Up button", "the price $25".

    07

    Confirm

    A change is read again three minutes later. Gone means a glitch, and nobody is told.

    08

    Judge

    Claude says what changed and whether it matters, following your note for the page.

    09

    Tell

    Important changes straight away; the rest in a daily round-up. Your ratings teach it.
