Video Studio · staff only

The render engine, tested on the worker.

Each test is queued here and rendered by the worker service, the same way every video will be. The page refreshes while a test is waiting or rendering.

What the worker has

 

Run a test

Test videos renders a 10-second landscape video (title, image, end card) and a 45-second vertical one with a chart. Outside-address drill renders a video that tries to load an image and a request from the internet: it must finish without them, and its log must name both addresses. Time-limit drill renders a page that never finishes loading: it must be stopped at its 60-second limit. Load run queues three videos at once, the longest 60 seconds; watch the worker's memory in Railway while they render.

Failure drills

Each drill makes one video that meets a failure on the live worker. Open it to see what a person would see, then use its buttons (Try again) to check it recovers. Website never answers takes about a minute and then plans without the site. Claude unreachable and Budget used up stop at the plan with a plain message. Render fails runs out of time on purpose; Try again renders it properly. A restart mid-render and bad uploads are drilled by hand (docs/video-studio-plan.md, section 7).
