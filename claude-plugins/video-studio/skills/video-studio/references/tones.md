# Tones

Ten presets. Each changes writing, typography, pacing, motion, transitions, music and SFX.
Presets are defaults, not limits; a freeform direction refines or overrides them and is kept
verbatim in the plan. Track ids refer to `assets/music/LIBRARY.md`.

---

## `default`
**Energy:** playful, clean, postable. The product is allowed to be funny on its own terms.
**Voice:** first person plural, warm, direct.
**Type:** mixed case, comfortable weight, words breathe.
**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes of 3-5s.
**Hook:** a simple observation that sets up the reveal. `Dating apps were built for humans. / Obvious mistake.`
**Highlights:** one short, punchy phrase per scene. `Swipe through eligible horses near your pasture.`
**Outro:** name, then the tagline. `Horse Tinder. / Find your perfect stablemate.`
**Transitions:** cut on the bar; clean slides; the odd crossfade.
**Music:** `upbeat-business-1`, `uplifting-pop`, `playful-sunny`. Natural ending when it fits.
**SFX:** 3-5, motion-matched: drops for pop-ins, soft impact on the reveal, a bell for the payoff.

## `polished`
**Energy:** serious, elegant; restraint is the creative choice.
**Voice:** third person or none; the product speaks.
**Type:** mixed case, light-to-medium weight, generous tracking, nothing aggressive.
**Pacing:** 3-4 scenes of 4-6s; slow reveals.
**Hook:** one strong image, or the name at full scale.
**Highlights:** one feature per scene, no lists.
**Outro:** name, tagline, stillness.
**Transitions:** slow crossfade (0.6-0.8s), slide; cuts still on the bar.
**Music:** `steady-business-12`, `corporate-imagefilm`, `uplifting-pop` low. Natural ending.
**SFX:** 2-3, very soft: `interface/drop_001`, `interface/bong_001`. Nothing aggressive.

## `yc-parody`
**Energy:** deadpan startup launch; the joke is how seriously it is delivered.
**Voice:** matter-of-fact, no winking.
**Type:** sentence case, medium-heavy; monospace for data points; no decoration.
**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes; one claim each.
**Hook:** the problem stated completely seriously. `Every day, taxis carry us. / But who carries the taxis?`
**Highlights:** features and metrics stated as fact (from the parody site's own claims). `Available in 12 metros.`
**Outro:** name, tagline, a URL that implies legitimacy.
**Transitions:** hard cuts on the bar; at most 0.2s crossfades.
**Music:** `warm-business-11`, `laidback-business-9`, `corporate-imagefilm`, at a polite level.
**SFX:** 2-3 dry cues: one soft impact on the reveal, one UI accent, one logo hit.

## `chaotic`
**Energy:** fast, loud, unhinged. The video is the joke.
**Voice:** short words, CAPS, metric dumps; confidence over exclamation marks.
**Type:** ALL CAPS, heavy, oversized; some words tilted or larger than expected.
**Pacing:** 6-9 scenes, some under 2s, none over 4s. Cuts every beat or every two beats.
**Hook:** something that should not exist, at full volume. `TRANSPORTATION WAS TOO CALM.`
**Highlights:** one word or number per beat. `8,400 BOARS / 3 MINUTE ETA / TUSKS-FIRST PICKUP`
**Outro:** the name slams in on the last hit; cut to black.
**Transitions:** hard cut, flash frame, zoom cut (1.2 → 1.0), whip.
**Music:** `bold-tropical-house` (from the drop), `punchy-business-10`. Button ending.
**SFX:** dense, stacked on beats: punches, glitches, dice, metal. Still not on every frame.

## `deadpan`
**Energy:** calm, dry; nothing registers as unusual.
**Voice:** one observation, then the product.
**Type:** large, sparse, lots of empty space, one thought at a time.
**Pacing:** 3-4 scenes of 4-7s; the pace is the joke.
**Hook:** a quiet observation, no setup. `I used to fear the sky.`
**Highlights:** one sentence per scene, no excitement. `Now I fear birds, weather, and gravity.`
**Outro:** the name. Maybe a tiny tagline. A long hold on empty space.
**Transitions:** very slow crossfades (0.8-1.0s) or long holds.
**Music:** `steady-business-12` or `minimal-morning-light` low (bed volume 0.35-0.5). Silence only
when the plan says silence is the joke.
**SFX:** 1-2 dry cues at most.

## `cinematic`
**Energy:** dramatic, trailer-scale; the product treated as a blockbuster.
**Voice:** epic, short declarative sentences; each lands before the next.
**Type:** ALL CAPS or heavy mixed case; full-bleed scenes; big scale changes.
**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes of 3-5s; reveals, not quick cuts.
**Hook:** a sweeping statement about the world. `For too long, / fish were told to stay underwater.`
**Highlights:** capabilities as superpowers. `Thermal identification. / Cloud navigation.`
**Outro:** the name full-screen on the final hit; tagline; the music rings out.
**Transitions:** dramatic wipe, slow crossfade with scale (0.95 → 1.0), light leak.
**Music:** `cinematic-brave`: its rebuild from 1:19 to the lift at 1:35 is a ready-made trailer;
anchor that lift to the reveal. Natural ending on its final hit.
**SFX:** 2-3 big ones: deep bell on the hero moment, soft impact on the reveal, bell on the outro.

## `app-store`
**Energy:** clean, professional, feature-forward.
**Voice:** feature-benefit, present tense.
**Type:** title case, medium weight, very readable.
**Pacing:** 4-6 scenes; each a feature name plus 1-2 details.
**Hook:** name + tagline, clean.
**Highlights:** feature cards. `Prompt Trauma Processing / Identify and resolve harmful conversation patterns.`
**Outro:** call to action. `Available now.`
**Transitions:** clean slide or wipe (0.35-0.45s).
**Music:** `uplifting-pop`, `warm-business-11`, `upbeat-business-1`.
**SFX:** a light, consistent layer: one drop or click per card (0.3-0.4 volume), bell on the outro.

## `documentary`
**Energy:** human, warm, honest; real people and places, nothing staged-looking.
**Voice:** first person from the people, or a quiet third person.
**Type:** a humanist serif or sans, mixed case, modest sizes over big photos; quotes verbatim.
**Pacing:** 3-5 scenes of 4-8s; let faces and hands hold.
**Hook:** a face, a place or a hand at work, with one line. `Every gift from the forest has three shares.`
**Highlights:** the craft, the people, the result, each a photograph with a line.
**Outro:** the brand, the offer, the action, quietly.
**Transitions:** soft crossfades, slow push-ins; cuts on the bar of a slow track.
**Music:** `warm-acoustic`, `documentary-hopeful`. Natural ending (both fade out by themselves).
**SFX:** almost none; maybe one soft wood knock on the end card.

## `luxury`
**Energy:** slow, minimal, rich; every frame could be a print ad.
**Voice:** few words, often fragments. `Hand-finished. / In Florence.`
**Type:** a high-contrast serif or a thin wide sans; wide tracking; small sizes are allowed here
because there is so little text (still above the minimums).
**Pacing:** 3-4 scenes of 4-6s; one slow camera move each.
**Hook:** a detail, extremely close, in perfect light.
**Highlights:** material, detail, the object whole.
**Outro:** the wordmark, alone, centred, held.
**Transitions:** long crossfades, dips to the brand colour.
**Music:** `cinematic-brave` (quiet passages), `steady-business-12` low, or `minimal-morning-light`.
**SFX:** none, or one soft glass or bell accent.

## `editorial`
**Energy:** magazine; type and photography on a strong grid.
**Voice:** headline writing: short, sharp, a little wit.
**Type:** a display serif with a mono or grotesque for labels; big numbers; rules and folios.
**Pacing:** 4-6 scenes; layouts change on the bar like page turns.
**Hook:** a cover: a big photograph, a headline, a folio.
**Highlights:** spreads: a photo, a pull quote, a stat, a caption.
**Outro:** the masthead-style wordmark.
**Transitions:** page-turn slides, hard cuts, grid wipes.
**Music:** `corporate-imagefilm`, `laidback-business-9`, `uplifting-pop`.
**SFX:** sparse paper and card sounds (`casino/card-slide-*` for page moves), one soft impact.
