# Outcomes Digital: education campaign launch videos

Two 16:9 videos (1920×1080, 30fps) for paid Meta / YouTube placements, made with
/brag-slim from edu.outcomes.digital and the Meta creative brief
(`outcomes-digital-education-creative-brief.docx`).

## What it is

- **What:** Outcomes Digital runs education marketing for schools, colleges and PG
  institutions. It is one connected system, from first enquiry to fee payment.
- **For:** school owners, principals, trustees and college admissions heads.
- **What sets it apart:** campaigns are run to cost per *admission*, not cost per
  form fill, and admissions data goes back into Google and Meta.
- **Sharpest claim:** "More enquiries isn't the goal." The real goal is more campus
  visits, applications and enrolments.
- **Visual hook:** a withheld number. The thing the viewer doesn't track is blurred
  or redacted, then revealed in orange. This is the brief's own "open a loop, close
  a loop" device, and in a video it can actually move.
- **Real UI reused:** the site's logo mark (SVG), its enquiry → campus visit →
  application → fee payment stepper, the rotating hero line, the five "How We Work"
  pillars, its mono eyebrow labels and its square orange buttons.
- **Tone:** `polished`, with app-store energy: confident and clean, with sharp,
  beat-led motion. It doesn't joke; the audience runs marketing themselves.
- **Share caption:** "You know what a lead costs. Do you know what an admission
  costs?"

## Guardrails from the brief (all respected)

- No numbers, percentages or results anywhere on screen.
- No promised or guaranteed admissions. The lines describe how they work.
- No stock photos of children: only type and interface visuals.
- No competitor (or client) institutions are named or shown.
