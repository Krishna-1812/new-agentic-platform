Frequently Asked Questions

What orthodontic treatments are offered at Selma, NC, Orthodontics?

At our office, we provide all the necessary treatments, including traditional metal and ceramic braces, Invisalign® clear aligners, retainers for maintenance, and more!

How do I take care of my braces and teeth during treatment?

Caring for your braces is highly important to maintaining proper oral hygiene. Therefore, we will provide you with a special care kit with the necessary tools to take care of your braces properly. We also encourage you to brush your teeth after every meal to keep your smile bright and healthy.

How often do I need to visit Selma, NC, Orthodontics during treatment?

For braces, you will typically visit every 4–8 weeks, while for Invisalign®, visits are usually every 6–10 weeks. For post-treatment retainers, visits are around 3–6 months to ensure proper maintenance.

Do you accept dental insurance or membership plans for orthodontics?

We accept and participate with a large majority of insurance plans. Before any treatment, our team will perform a complimentary benefits evaluation to let you know what your insurance covers. We also offer flexible payment options and an affordable dental membership plan for patients without insurance.
