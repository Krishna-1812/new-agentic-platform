Comprehensive & compassionate dentistry for the whole family

We Treat All Ages

We treat patients of all ages through our multiple general dentist practices and dedicated pediatric and orthodontic practices.

Multi-Specialty Dental Practice

We have dedicated doctors to provide orthodontics, pediatrics, periodontics, and endodontics at most of our locations.

Broad Network of Insurance Accepted

We accept the majority of all major insurance plans as well as many regional plans. For those without insurance, take advantage of our in-house discount plan.

Convenient Appointments

With multiple locations throughout Central, Eastern, and Western Virginia, we provide convenient appointments and an experience that exceeds expectations.

Endodontics

Protect your teeth and relieve pain with our expert endodontic care. We specialize in root canals and other treatments to save your natural teeth and maintain your oral health.

General Dentistry

The best way to keep your smile healthy is through routine appointments and fixing issues before they progress. We offer preventive, periodontal, and restorative treatments to help you maintain that beautiful smile.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Not everyone is born with a smile they cherish — but everyone deserves one. At our offices, you’ll find modern cosmetic dental services to fine-tune your smile and boost your confidence.

Learn More

Orthodontics

Straight teeth don’t just look great, they ensure a good bite. We offer options for kids and adults alike at our orthodontic offices, including traditional braces and Clarity Aligners.

Learn More

Pediatric Dentistry

Give your kids the gift of a healthy and beautiful smile. At our pediatric offices, we offer specialized care for ages 0-18. We’ll make sure your lil’ ones’ smile is healthy and help them develop positive habits into adulthood.

Periodontics

Periodontal treatment focuses on gum health and supporting structures around the teeth. Our periodontist can help you diagnose and treat gum disease and maintain oral well-being.
